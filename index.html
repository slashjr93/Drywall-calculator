How Does the Drywall Calculator Work?

Our drywall calculator estimates the amount of drywall needed based on your room dimensions. Enter the length, width, and wall height of the room, then choose your drywall sheet size.

The calculator estimates the wall area and can also include the ceiling. Doors and windows are deducted from the total area, and a waste factor is added to help account for cuts, mistakes, and unusable pieces.

How Many Sheets of Drywall Do I Need?

The number of drywall sheets depends on the total square footage of your walls and ceilings and the size of the drywall sheets. A standard 4 × 8 foot sheet covers 32 square feet.
